# SUODE Medical International Website

Professional endoscopic medical equipment manufacturer website for global healthcare providers.

## Overview
This is the international website for Hangzhou SUODE Medical Equipment Co., Ltd., featuring their range of endoscopic medical equipment including gynecology, urology, and surgical endoscopes.

## Pages
- **index.html** - Home page with hero section, company overview, and product categories
- **products.html** - Product catalog with gynecology, urology, and camera systems
- **about.html** - Company story, mission, vision, and certifications
- **support.html** - After-sales service and technical support information
- **contact.html** - Contact form and inquiry submission

## Tech Stack
- HTML5
- Tailwind CSS (via CDN)
- Font Awesome Icons (via CDN)
- No build step required

## Deployment
This site is deployed on Vercel.
