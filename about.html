About SUODE Medical

Dedicated to advancing healthcare through innovative endoscopic solutions

Our Story

Hangzhou SUODE Medical Equipment Co., Ltd. was established in Tonglu Economic Development Zone, Zhejiang Province, strategically located along the beautiful Fuchun River. Our facility spans over 10,000 square meters with state-of-the-art manufacturing equipment.

As a high-tech enterprise, we integrate research, development, production, and distribution of medical devices. Our team of 180+ professionals includes experienced engineers, quality control specialists, and customer service representatives dedicated to excellence.

For over 20 years, we have been committed to providing healthcare professionals worldwide with high-quality, reliable endoscopic equipment. Our mission is to "create innovative products that meet China's medical market needs" while expanding our reach to serve global healthcare providers.

Our Mission

To develop innovative medical devices that address clinical needs, improve patient outcomes, and advance healthcare delivery through the integration of industry, academia, and research.

Our Vision

To become a leading global manufacturer of endoscopic medical equipment, recognized for technological innovation, product quality, and exceptional service in the medical device industry.
