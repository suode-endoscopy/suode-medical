About SUODE Medical

Hangzhou SUODE Medical Equipment Co., Ltd. is a high-tech enterprise specializing in the research, development, production, and distribution of medical devices. Located in Tonglu Economic Development Zone, Zhejiang Province, along the beautiful Fuchun River.

Our products cover laparoscopy, thoracoscopy, gynecology, general surgery, ENT, urology, and more. With diverse varieties, complete specifications, and innovative designs, we provide excellent after-sales service. Our products are widely trusted by medical institutions across China.
